# JobHunter

> **Save time. Make better career decisions.**

JobHunter is an AI-powered job intelligence assistant designed to reduce the time spent searching, reading and evaluating job opportunities.

Instead of manually reviewing hundreds of job descriptions, JobHunter automatically collects, filters, analyzes and prioritizes opportunities so you can focus on what really matters:

> **Getting hired.**

---

# The Problem

Finding job opportunities is easy.

Finding the **right** opportunities isn't.

Professionals spend hours every week:

- Reading long job descriptions
- Comparing requirements
- Trying to understand whether a role is actually a good fit
- Applying to positions with little chance of success

Most of that time is wasted.

JobHunter exists to eliminate that waste.

---

# Why I Built This

I built JobHunter after realizing that the hardest part of job searching wasn't finding opportunities, it was deciding which ones actually deserved my time.

Reading dozens of long job descriptions every day became repetitive, mentally exhausting and inefficient.

As a Technical Operations professional with a passion for automation and AI, I decided to build the tool I wished existed.

JobHunter automates the repetitive work, summarizes what's important and helps me focus on the opportunities that truly matter.

# The Goal

JobHunter doesn't try to find more jobs.

It helps you make better decisions about the jobs you've already found.

Its mission is simple:

- Read less.
- Understand faster.
- Apply with confidence.

---

# Philosophy

Every feature must save time.

If a feature doesn't reduce the time, effort or uncertainty involved in finding a better job, it doesn't belong in the project.

Technology is not the goal.

Helping people make faster and better career decisions is.

# How It Works

```
Collect Jobs
      │
      ▼
Rule-Based Filtering
      │
      ▼
AI Analysis
      │
      ▼
Prioritized Recommendations
      │
      ▼
```

JobHunter combines deterministic rules with Large Language Models to produce recommendations that are both explainable and context-aware.
