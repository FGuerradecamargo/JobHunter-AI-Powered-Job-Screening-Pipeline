      Automation Engineer | Biopharmaceutical Manufacturing


      Our client, a global leader in biopharmaceutical manufacturing, is looking for an Automation Engineer to join a high-performing engineering team supporting cutting-edge manufacturing and laboratory operations.

      This is an excellent opportunity to work in a highly automated GMP environment, playing a key role in maintaining, improving, and delivering reliable automation systems that support life-changing products.


      Key Responsibilities

      • Provide technical support for manufacturing and laboratory automation systems.
      • Troubleshoot and resolve automation issues to minimise downtime and maximise performance.
      • Support the commissioning, qualification, and startup of new equipment and automation systems.
      • Lead or support automation upgrades, system improvements, and integration projects.
      • Manage automation-related change controls, investigations, deviations, and CAPAs.
      • Work closely with Manufacturing, Quality, Validation, Engineering, and IT teams.
      • Ensure automation systems remain compliant with GMP, data integrity, and regulatory requirements.
      • Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance system reliability, efficiency, and performance.


      Requirements

      • Degree in Automation, Electrical, Electronic, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
      • 3+ years' automation engineering experience within pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical device, or another regulated manufacturing environment.
      • Hands-on experience supporting manufacturing automation and/or laboratory systems.
      • Good understanding of GMP, change control, and validation practices.
      • Strong troubleshooting and root cause analysis skills.
      • Excellent communication skills and the ability to work across multidisciplinary teams.

      • Seniority level

        Associate

      • Employment type

        Full-time

      • Job function

        Engineering and Manufacturing

      • Industries

        Pharmaceutical Manufacturing and Manufacturing
